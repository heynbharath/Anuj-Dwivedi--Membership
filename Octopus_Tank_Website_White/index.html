Questions

Quick answers

Who can take part?

Students of DSU School of Engineering only, in B.Tech and M.Tech programmes.

How big is a team?

Two to four members per team.

What does it cost?

There is a per-team RSVP fee of ₹299. It is asked for only when your team registers through the registration form, so there is nothing to pay before that.

When is it?

31 October 2026. The day starts with a two-hour pitch workshop, followed by live startup pitching.

Which domains can we pitch in?

AI & Data, Cybersecurity, Robotics & IoT, Engineering Design, Sustainability, Aero & Mobility, Business & Legal Tech, and MedTech.

What can we win?

Cash prizes, internship opportunities, mentorship and startup guidance.

How do we register?

Your team registers through the registration form. We will publish the link here. The RSVP fee is requested at that step. Until then, message us on WhatsApp with any questions.
